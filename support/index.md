# Holy Hunger Support


Holy Hunger is run by Rooftop Labs LLC. We're glad to help.

## Contact us
Email **rooftoplabsllc@gmail.com**. We reply within 2 business days.

## Subscriptions
- **Manage or cancel:** open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name,
  then Subscriptions, then Holy Hunger. Subscriptions renew automatically
  until you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
- **Restore a purchase:** in Holy Hunger, tap "I already have a subscription"
  on the welcome screen, or Settings > Restore purchases.
- **Refunds:** Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at
  https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

## Health & safety
Holy Hunger offers spiritual guidance, not medical advice. Fasting isn't
right for everyone. Talk to your doctor before fasting, especially if you are
pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes or another medical condition, take
medication, or have a history of disordered eating. Stop fasting and seek
care if you feel faint, confused, or unwell.

Your health-check answers stay on your iPhone. To change them, open
Settings > Health & safety > Retake the health check.
