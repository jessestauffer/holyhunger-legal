# Holy Hunger Privacy Policy


**Effective date:** November 10, 2026

Holy Hunger helps you plan and keep a Christian fast with daily scripture and
prayer. Holy Hunger is run by Rooftop Labs LLC ("we", "us"). We built it to know as little about you as possible. This policy
explains what the app stores, where it is stored, and who else sees it.

## The short version
- Your health-check answers never leave your phone.
- Your fast plan, progress, and settings are stored on your phone. A few
  non-health details (like the type and length of your fast) are shared with
  the services described below.
- Apple handles payments. We never see your card details.
- We don't show ads, and we don't sell or share your data with advertisers.

## Your health-check answers stay on your phone
Before you choose a fast, Holy Hunger asks a few health questions (for
example, pregnancy or a condition like diabetes). It also asks whether a doctor
has cleared you to fast. The app uses your answers only to decide which fasts
to offer you.

- Your answers are stored **only on your device**.
- They are **never sent** to us, to our analytics, to Superwall, or to anyone else.
- They are never written to logs, and they are excluded from iCloud and
  device backups.
- We don't ask for proof that a doctor cleared you. Your answer stays on your device.
- You can change your answers at any time in Settings > Health & safety.
  Deleting the app deletes them.

## Other information the app stores
Your fast plan (type, length, start date), your daily progress, the reason you
gave for fasting, and your reminder settings are stored on your device so the
app can guide you. Deleting the app deletes the copy on your device. Some of
these details are also shared with Superwall and with our usage analytics, as
explained in the next sections. Your health-check answers never are.

## Purchases
Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple
handles your payment details under its own privacy policy
(https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We receive confirmation of whether you
have an active subscription, but not your name, email, or payment details.
You can manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone's Settings.

## Superwall (subscription screen)
We use Superwall to show the subscription screen. Superwall receives your
subscription status and device information such as app version, device model,
language, and region, plus a few onboarding choices that personalize the
screen: the type and length of fast you chose, the reason you gave for
fasting, and which research statistic you were shown. Superwall **never**
receives your health-check answers. Superwall's privacy policy:
https://superwall.com/privacy

## Analytics
The app records usage events, such as "onboarding step viewed", "fast
started", or "day completed", so we can see where the app is confusing and
improve it. Some events include details about your fast, such as its type,
length, and day number, and the answers you give to non-health onboarding
questions. These events never contain your health-check answers, your name,
or your contact details.

## Notifications
If you allow notifications, Holy Hunger schedules one daily reminder on your
device for that day's scripture and prayer. Notifications are scheduled
locally. We don't send them from a server, and turning them off in Settings
stops them.

## Children
Holy Hunger is not directed at children. The health check does not offer food
fasts to anyone under 18. Users under 18 are offered non-food fasts only
(for example, a fast from your phone).

## Your choices and rights
Your health-check answers live only on your device: change them in Settings,
or delete the app to remove them. To ask us to delete the usage and
subscription-screen data described above, email us. For
any privacy question or request, including requests under laws such as the
GDPR or CCPA, email us at rooftoplabsllc@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

## Changes to this policy
If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. If a change
affects how we handle your information, we'll tell you in the app before it
applies.
